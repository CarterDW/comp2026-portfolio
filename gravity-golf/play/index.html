Play gravity golf

One launch per attempt: an angle and a speed, then gravity takes over. Planets bend the ball's path, and touching one is a crash. These are the held-out courses the network was tested on. On half of the planet holes it sinks the ball in one shot, and on the other half it misses. Can you do better? You can also watch its shot, and the shot an optimizer found after many tries.

Trails: you the network the optimizer · faint lines: your earlier attempts (gold sunk, orange crash, blue out of bounds)
